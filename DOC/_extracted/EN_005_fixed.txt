1. Getting Started
NexusFinance is your digital lending platform. You can access it from any web browser or install the Android app for the best experience.
2. Creating an Account
2.1 Registration
Go to https://nexusfinancefintech.vercel.app
Click 'Register'
Enter your name, email, and password
Click 'Register'
Check your email for a 6-digit verification code
Enter the code in the verification form
Your account is now active — you can log in
2.2 Login
Enter your email and password
Click 'Login'
You will be redirected to your dashboard
Email not verified?
New accounts must verify their email before the first login. If your email isn't verified yet, you'll be asked to enter the 6-digit code emailed to you during registration — log in again once it's confirmed.
Forgot your password?
Click 'Forgot Password' on the login page. Enter your email, receive an OTP code, verify it, then set a new password.
3. Applying for a Loan
3.1 Start Application
Click 'Apply for Loan' from the sidebar or dashboard.
3.2 Fill in Details
Personal Information (name, email, phone)
Loan Type (SME Loan, Personal Loan, etc.)
Loan Amount and Duration
Purpose of the loan
Monthly income information
3.3 Submit
Review your details and click 'Submit Application'. Your application will be reviewed by a Loan Officer.
4. Making Payments
4.1 KHQR Payment
Navigate to 'KHQR Payment' in the sidebar to generate a QR code for repayment.
Enter the payment amount
Select the currency (USD or KHR)
A KHQR code will be generated
Scan with your banking app (Bakong-compatible)
Confirm the payment
4.2 View Transactions
Check 'History Logs' to see all your past transactions and payments.
5. Managing Your Profile
Navigate to 'Profile' in the sidebar to update your information:
Name, email, and phone number
Change your password (requires OTP verification)
View your role and account status
6. Frequently Asked Questions
Question
Answer
How long does loan approval take?
Typically 1-3 business days after submission.
What payment methods are accepted?
KHQR (Bakong) and ABA PayWay.
Can I apply for multiple loans?
Check with your loan officer for policy details.
How do I link my Telegram account?
Send /link to the bot, then confirm with the code emailed to you (/confirm <code>).
