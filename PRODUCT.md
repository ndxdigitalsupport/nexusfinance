# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary surface is a marketing landing page targeting:
- **Microfinance institutions** and lending businesses in Cambodia / Southeast Asia evaluating a digital lending platform
- **Loan officers** and branch managers researching operational tools
- **Business owners** seeking digital payment, KYC, and analytics solutions

Secondary surface: the fintech dashboard app serving three portals (Customer, Loan Officer, Super Admin) who use the platform daily to manage loans, payments, and compliance.

## Product Purpose

NexusFinance provides an all-in-one FinTech ecosystem for Southeast Asian lending operations — unifying digital wallets, business lending, payment gateways, analytics dashboards, payroll/HR finance, and compliance/KYC into a single platform. The landing page communicates trust, capability, and modernity to convince businesses to adopt the platform.

Success looks like: visitors understand the full ecosystem in under 30 seconds, explore services, and submit their email for sandbox access.

## Brand Personality

secure, modern, cutting-edge

The voice is confident and authoritative — not playful or casual. Tone leans professional with moments of warmth through human-centric messaging ("Made Finance Simple with You"). Design communicates technical capability + approachability.

## Anti-references

No specific anti-references. The goal is to look good and modern — avoid generic AI-slop patterns: card-grid fatigue, tiny uppercase eyebrows on every section, gradient text, glassmorphism as default, side-stripe borders.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust through restraint** — A fintech landing page must feel secure. Clean layout, controlled palette, deliberate whitespace. Nothing flashy that undermines credibility.
2. **Show capability, not features** — Each service is demonstrated with concrete benefits and metrics, not just feature lists. The dashboard mockup in the hero proves the product is real.
3. **Bilingual by default** — English and Khmer are equal citizens. The design must accommodate both scripts without compromise at any breakpoint.
4. **Motion with purpose** — Animations guide attention and convey polish, never distract. Scroll reveals, hover micro-interactions, and the live dashboard badge all serve the narrative of a working platform.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target (4.5:1 body contrast minimum). Dark mode default with light mode toggle. Khmer language support with adjusted typography (line-height, letter-spacing, scale compensation) to prevent ligature clipping. Reduced motion alternative via `prefers-reduced-motion` media query.
