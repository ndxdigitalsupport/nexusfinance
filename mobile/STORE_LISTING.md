## Google Play Store

### Short Description (max 80 chars)
Smart loan management platform for Cambodia — apply, track, and repay loans.

### Full Description (max 4000 chars)
NexusFinance is a modern digital lending platform designed for the Cambodian financial ecosystem. Whether you're a borrower looking for quick loan access or a lending officer managing applications, NexusFinance provides a seamless, secure experience.

**For Borrowers:**
• Apply for loans directly from your phone — quick, simple, paperless
• Track your loan status, repayment schedule, and outstanding balance in real time
• Make payments via KHQR scan or ABA PayWay integration
• Receive payment reminders and notifications via Telegram
• View your complete loan history and transaction records

**For Lending Officers & Administrators:**
• Review, approve, or reject loan applications on the go
• Manage borrower KYC documents and compliance tasks
• Send broadcasts and reminders to clients
• Access real-time platform statistics and reports

**Security & Compliance:**
• Bank-grade security with encrypted data storage
• OTP verification for sensitive operations
• Role-based access controls
• Compliant with Cambodian financial regulations

**Payment Integration:**
• KHQR — scan to pay with any Bakong-enabled banking app
• ABA PayWay — secure online payment processing
• Real-time payment confirmation and receipt generation

NexusFinance is built for Cambodia, supporting Khmer language and local payment infrastructure. Whether you're in Phnom Penh or the provinces, manage your finances from anywhere.
